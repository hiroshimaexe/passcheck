Enter a password to check its strength

    Length matters

    Use at least 12 characters. Each extra character makes a password exponentially harder to guess.

    Mix characters

    Combine uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols to widen the range of possibilities.

    Avoid patterns

    Common words, sequences like 123 or qwerty, and repeated characters are the first things attackers try.
